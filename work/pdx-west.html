PDX West Logistics Center

A leasing flyer and site signage for a 938,000 SF Class A distribution building on Portland’s Airport Way.

[Type]Leasing Flyer
[Location]Portland, OR
[Year]2026
[Role][[Lauren to confirm]]
[01]Project

PDX West Logistics Center is a planned 938,000 SF Class A, cross-loaded distribution building at NE Marine Drive and NE 33rd Drive — Airport Way’s largest available Class A industrial space, half a mile from Portland International Airport, breaking ground in spring 2027. Colliers and developer Capstone Partners needed a leasing flyer that could sell a building still on the drawing board to logistics and distribution tenants and the brokers who represent them: make the scale and specifications legible at a glance, prove the location, and look as considered as the Class A product it describes.

[02]Solution

A landscape flyer built on architectural renderings and aerial photography, cut through with oversized, translucent sage-green triangles and anchored by a heavy, wide all-caps display face for the PDX West wordmark and section titles. A restrained palette of sage, slate and white keeps ten information-dense pages calm and recognisably one family, and the cover art carries straight onto the site signage, so the building, the flyer and the sign read as one brand.

The story moves from the building outward: property highlights set out as a two-column specification sheet under a single headline claim, then the site plan, corporate neighbors, the Airport Way submarket, a location map, the regional logistics hubs, workforce statistics and a West Coast drive-time map. Each page pairs one strong image or map with one tight block of facts, so a broker can find clear height, dock doors, trailer stalls or drive distances in seconds.
