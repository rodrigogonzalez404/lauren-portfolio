Springwood of Beaverton

A digital offering memorandum for a 55+ manufactured-home community in Beaverton.

[Client]Colliers
[Type]Digital Offering Memorandum
[Location]Beaverton, OR
[Year]2026
[Role]Graphic Designer
[01]Project

Springwood of Beaverton is an age-restricted (55+) manufactured-home community on 14.57 acres in Washington County: 74 multi-section homes and one apartment attached to the clubhouse, 100% occupied and made up entirely of tenant-owned homes. Colliers was taking it to market through a call for offers, and needed an offering memorandum that would give investors the full picture — the asset, its value-add story and its location near some of Oregon’s largest employers — in a document as orderly as the community itself.

[02]Solution

A landscape memorandum in charcoal, deep teal and white, with aerial photography of the community and a vintage-style Springwood badge that carries from the cover to the back page. Arboria’s rounded geometric headlines and light numerals keep a data-heavy document open and approachable, and fine diagonal hatching adds texture to the cover.

The document is organized into five numbered sections — Executive Summary, Property Overview, Financial Overview, Market Comparables and Location Overview — each opening on a full-bleed teal divider over the aerial. Property highlights become a grid of icons, the details read as a clean spec table, and a site plan, photo spreads of the homes and clubhouse, an annotated aerial and the MSA, employment and Oregon rent-control pages build the investment case.
