Nomad

An investor offering package that sells a North Portland apartment community through bold colour, editorial photography and clear market storytelling.

[Client]Colliers
[Type]Investor Offering Package
[Location]Overlook, North Portland, OR
[Year]2026
[Role]Graphic Designer
[01]Project

An apartment community in North Portland’s Overlook neighborhood, brought to market by Colliers as an investment sale. The offering package had to give investors a fast, credible read and stand out in a crowded pipeline.

[02]Solution

Five numbered chapters, each opening on a full-bleed photo washed in a red-to-yellow gradient. Heavy italic type, a four-colour palette, a consistent icon set and charts in the same colours keep every page reading at a glance.
