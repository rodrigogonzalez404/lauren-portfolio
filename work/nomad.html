[01]Project

Nomad is an apartment community in North Portland’s Overlook neighborhood, brought to market by Colliers as an investment sale. The offering package had to give institutional and private investors a fast, credible read on the property, the neighborhood and the Portland market — and make the opportunity stand out in a crowded deal pipeline.

[02]Solution

A landscape document organized into five numbered chapters — Investment Summary, Property Description, Market Overview, The Comparables and The Financials. Each chapter opens on a full-bleed photograph washed in a red-to-yellow colour gradient, with a charcoal chapter tab and an oversized italic numeral.

A tight system carries every page: heavy italic display type and orange headlines in outlined title boxes, a four-colour palette of signal red, orange, sun yellow and charcoal, diagonal hatch accents, a consistent icon set, a custom site plan, neighborhood and access maps, and charts drawn in the same palette so market data reads at a glance.
