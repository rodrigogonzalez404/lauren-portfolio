Madison Plaza

An investor offering package for a 157-unit apartment community next to Kent Station.

[Client]Colliers
[Type]Investor Offering Package
[Location]Kent, WA
[Year]2026
[Role]Graphic Designer
[01]Project

Madison Plaza is a 157-unit apartment community built in 2022 at 102 Madison Avenue in downtown Kent, Washington, with ground-floor retail and the Kent Sounder Station next door. Colliers’ Capital Markets Group was bringing it to market as a core-plus investment and needed an offering memorandum that would make the case for its embedded revenue upside — transit access, a growing South King County economy and a renter base drawn to downtown Kent.

[02]Solution

A landscape memorandum in raspberry and plum charcoal on white, set entirely in Montserrat — thin, wide capitals for the headlines with the key words picked out in bold raspberry — and led by bright photography of the new building and its interiors. A numbered contents spread sets up four sections: Executive Summary, The Property, The Financials and The Market.

A consistent system keeps a data-heavy document readable: a clean offering-summary table, maps that place the property between Seattle, the airport and the Kent Valley industrial core, transit and connectivity tables, oversized figures for the region’s growth and its renters, and “The Kent Advantage”, which sets each fact beside what it means and why it matters.
