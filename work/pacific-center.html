Pacific Center

A leasing brochure for a Class A office tower half a block from Pioneer Courthouse Square.

[Client]Colliers
[Type]Leasing Brochure
[Location]Portland, OR
[Year]2026
[Role]Graphic Designer
[01]Project

Pacific Center at 851 SW Sixth Avenue is a Class A office tower in the heart of downtown Portland’s Central Business District — half a block from Pioneer Courthouse Square, directly on the MAX light rail and bus mall, and owned and managed by Schnitzer Properties. Colliers needed a leasing brochure to market suites from about 1,300 SF to nearly 16,000 SF to tenants and their brokers, and to show a building with the amenities of a modern workplace at one of the city’s most connected addresses.

[02]Solution

A tall-format brochure in deep navy with warm taupe panels: light, widely spaced Gotham capitals for the headlines, Adobe Caslon for the text, and a slim vertical folio running down each page edge. Photography leads every spread — the tower, the atrium lobby, the new employee lounge, the fitness center — framed by navy margins so the building always reads first.

The spreads move from the building to the street: the cover and the downtown story, the amenities, the highlights in a grid of line icons, then the neighborhood — walk, transit and bike scores of 99, 97 and 94 over a map of restaurants, shops and culture — before the availabilities, a sample floor plan with a spec furniture layout, and the leasing team on the back cover.
