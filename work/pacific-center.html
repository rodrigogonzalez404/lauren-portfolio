Pacific Center

A leasing brochure for a Class A office tower half a block from Pioneer Courthouse Square.

[Client]Colliers
[Type]Leasing Brochure
[Location]Portland, OR
[Year]2026
[Role]Graphic Designer
[01]Project

A Class A office tower in downtown Portland, half a block from Pioneer Courthouse Square and directly on the MAX light rail. Colliers needed a leasing brochure to market its suites to tenants and their brokers.

[02]Solution

A tall-format brochure in deep navy and warm taupe, with light Gotham capitals, Adobe Caslon text and photography leading every spread. It moves from the building and its amenities to the neighborhood, the availabilities, a sample floor plan and the leasing team.
