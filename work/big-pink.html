Big Pink

A Ceros pitch for Big Pink, the U.S. Bancorp Tower, and the identity it proposed — OnePink, from the logo to sample marketing material.

[Client]Colliers [[team — confirm]]
[Type]Pitch Presentation & Brand Concepts
[Format]Ceros interactive presentation
[Location]Portland, OR
[Year]2025
[Role]Designer — the full Ceros presentation, naming and logo concepts
[01]Project

Big Pink — the U.S. Bancorp Tower at 111 SW 5th Avenue, “the icon of downtown Portland” — had a new owner, who called owning it “deeply meaningful” and “also a responsibility.” Colliers pitched to lead the building’s leasing and marketing with an interactive presentation that had to show the tower “seen from a new angle”: its story, the market and the competition, and a marketing approach bold enough to reposition a landmark — down to a possible new name.

[02]Solution

The proposed identity, OnePink, joins the address and the nickname in one word. Wide-set, rounded capitals run vertically like the tower — ONE in white, PINK in rose on deep navy — with a four-colour palette chosen for what each colour says about the building and a tagline, “One City. One Workplace. One Big Experience.” It is carried through sample marketing material: a brochure, a flyer, a tablet and a phone.

It sits inside an interactive Ceros presentation in eight numbered chapters — Introductions, The Building, The Market, The Competition, Marketing Approach, Fee Proposal, Why Us? and Appendix. Each chapter opens on a deep navy panel with a rose-pink outlined numeral beside full-bleed photography of the tower and the city; a vertical BIG PINK wordmark frames the cover and the close. The marketing approach builds up to the identity through a moodboard, sixteen sample names from standard to unique, and three logo ideas — OneEleven, Big Pink and OnePink — each shown over the skyline, in dimensional letters and on building signage.
