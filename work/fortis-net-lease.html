[01]Project

Fortis Net Lease, a national real estate brokerage, needed stronger, more cohesive design assets to support their growth and reflect their position in a competitive, fast-changing market.

[02]Solution

Since 2018, I’ve worked hand-in-hand with Fortis Net Lease’s Marketing Manager to strengthen their visual identity through hundreds of custom design assets.

From trade show booths and signage to email campaigns, presentation decks, and social media content, our collaboration ensured every touchpoint reflected a sharp, consistent, and professional aesthetic. Together, we’ve helped reinforce Fortis’s presence as a national leader in real estate investment.

[03]Results

During our collaboration, Fortis grew from $5.5B to over $10B in total sales. The upgraded visuals have helped strengthen their brand, support client trust, and position them as a market leader in investment real estate.
