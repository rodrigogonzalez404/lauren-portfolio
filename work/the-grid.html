The Grid (Kirkland 405)

A name and identity for a Kirkland office building, repositioned for the region’s innovation economy.

[Type]Naming & Brand Identity
[Location]Kirkland, WA
[Year]2025
[Role][[Lauren to confirm]]
[01]Project

Kirkland 405 is an office building in Kirkland, Washington, being repositioned to attract dynamic, innovation-focused tenants from the region’s growing ecosystem of AI, healthcare and creative technology companies. The Colliers team needed a name and an identity to lead that story — one that would feel modern, connected and tech-driven, and tie the building to the broader Kirkland and Seattle innovation story.

[02]Solution

A naming study that developed three names — The Grid, Stage One and The Pulse — all the way to a logo, each with its own mark, five key messages, the reasoning behind the name, and the mark shown in place on an exterior blade sign and a lobby feature wall. Every route shares one restrained presentation system, a single cool grey on warm stone with light Open Sans, so the client compares ideas, not styling.

The Grid is a nod to both the tech infrastructure that powers innovation and the urban connectivity of Seattle. Its mark pairs two offset, open square frames — a plan of connected space — with a light geometric wordmark whose open letters echo the gaps in the frames. Drawn as simple line work, it translates directly into dimensional letters, reverses cleanly onto dark panels, and reduces to the two frames alone.

[03]Results

The client chose The Grid as the building’s name and logo. In their feedback it stood out immediately as modern, connected and tech-driven — the right direction for repositioning the property for innovation-focused tenants — and they called the full set of concepts a strong creative foundation for telling the building’s story.

In place

The identity on the building — exterior signage and the lobby wall — and on a business card.

Concept exploration

Three names were developed all the way to a logo, each with its own key messages and the reasoning behind it, alongside a wider list that included Node113, 405Xchange and Kirkland Hub. The Grid was chosen; Stage One and The Pulse remain concepts.

Concept 01 · Chosen

The Grid

  • Connected
  • Tech Infrastructure
  • Urban Energy
  • Startup Ecosystem
  • Seattle Roots

The Grid is a nod to both the tech infrastructure that powers innovation and the urban connectivity of Seattle. It positions the building as a place where startups stay plugged into the city’s energy, resources, and talent. Whether you’re launching a new idea or scaling up, The Grid is where you stay connected.

Concept 02

Stage One

  • Foundation
  • Growth Platform
  • Startup Journey
  • Performance Space
  • Scalable

Stage One represents both a starting point and a platform for growth. It’s a place where businesses, whether at their first pitch or their hundredth hire, can thrive. The name also evokes a performance stage, giving tenants a space to shine, present, and lead. It’s aspirational, flexible, and empowering.

Concept 03

The Pulse

  • Vitality
  • Innovation Hub
  • Momentum
  • Creative Energy
  • Startup Culture

The Pulse symbolizes the heartbeat of innovation. It’s where energy flows, ideas spark, and businesses come to life. With its modern finishes and user-focused upgrades, the building becomes more than just a workspace, it’s a living hub for creativity and momentum.
