CGK Unlimited

CGK, a growing home and apparel brand, needed a design strategy to elevate its seasonal, children's, and year-round product lines.

[Client]CGK Unlimited
[Industry]Apparel + Retail
[Service]Pattern Design, Seasonal Design
[Year]2024
[01]Project

CGK, a growing home and apparel brand, needed a design strategy to elevate its seasonal, children's, and year-round product lines. The goal was to boost e-commerce performance and create pattern designs that would resonate with both new and returning customers.

[02]Solution

In close coordination with the Director of Product Development, I led the creation of over 60 original surface pattern designs that brought seasonal narratives, children's themes, and evergreen concepts to life.

Our ongoing exchange of insights, from market trends to production considerations, ensured each design not only resonated emotionally with customers but was also viable for large-scale manufacturing and aligned with broader brand goals.

[03]Results

The refreshed cohesive visual storytelling, and seasonal pattern strategy led to a 20% increase in overall sales, with several seasonal product lines selling out shortly after launch. Campaigns featuring the newly developed assets achieved conversion rates of up to 40%.
