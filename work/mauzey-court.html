A digital offering memorandum for a fully leased flex/industrial building in Hillsboro’s tech corridor.

[Client]Colliers
[Type]Digital Offering Memorandum
[Location]Hillsboro, OR
[Year]2026
[Role]Graphic Designer
[01]Project

A 20,000 SF flex/industrial building in Hillsboro, Oregon, fully leased to flavor and terpene manufacturer True Terpenes. Colliers needed an offering memorandum showing investors a turnkey, mission-critical asset in a strong tech submarket.

[02]Solution

Deep teal and seafoam with Rift’s condensed capitals, full-bleed aerials and interiors, and dot grids and thin rules for a precise, technical feel. It reads the way an investor works a deal: the asset, the tenant, a photo tour, the market and three access maps.

Design system

Rift Bold, tall and condensed, for the headlines; Proxima Nova for the text.
