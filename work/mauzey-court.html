8210 Mauzey Court

A digital offering memorandum for a fully leased flex/industrial building in Hillsboro’s tech corridor.

[Client]Colliers
[Type]Digital Offering Memorandum
[Location]Hillsboro, OR
[Year]2026
[Role]Graphic Designer
[01]Project

8210 Mauzey Court is a 20,000 SF flex/industrial building in Hillsboro, Oregon, 100% leased to True Terpenes — a flavor and terpene manufacturer whose 2021 buildout added high-end offices, production areas, clean rooms and a fully climate-controlled warehouse. Colliers was bringing it to market as an investment sale and needed an offering memorandum that would show investors a turnkey, mission-critical asset in one of the region’s strongest tech submarkets, home to Intel, Tokyo Electron and ASML.

[02]Solution

A landscape memorandum in deep teal and seafoam, with Rift’s tall, condensed capitals for the headlines and Proxima Nova for the text. Full-bleed aerials and interior photography sit beside solid colour panels, while small dot grids, thin rules and outlined circles give the pages a precise, technical rhythm that suits a clean-room manufacturer.

The document reads the way an investor works through a deal: the executive summary and property overview, six investment highlights with line icons, the tenant and the lease, a photo tour of the offices, production floor and warehouse, then the market — Portland and Washington County industrial data charted in the same palette — demographics, and three maps that prove regional, national and local access, before closing on the building and the brokers.

Design system

Rift Bold, tall and condensed, for the headlines; Proxima Nova for the text.
