The Leftbank Project

A digital leasing brochure for a historic creative-office block in the heart of Albina.

[Client]Colliers
[Type]Digital Leasing Brochure
[Location]Portland, OR
[Year]2026
[Role]Graphic Designer
[01]Project

A 1923 A.E. Doyle building, one of the last originals from Portland’s Albina neighborhood, now seismically retrofitted. Colliers needed a digital leasing brochure for its office and creative spaces that led with the building’s character and a neighborhood on the rise.

[02]Solution

Full-bleed photography of brick, timber and big windows, framed by black panels and a burnt-orange accent, with Corsario headlines and a strip of six amenity icons. The pages move from the building’s history and spaces out to a local-amenities map and the revitalization of Lower Albina.
