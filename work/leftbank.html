The Leftbank Project

A digital leasing brochure for a historic creative-office block in the heart of Albina.

[Client]Colliers
[Type]Digital Leasing Brochure
[Location]Portland, OR
[Year]2026
[Role]Graphic Designer
[01]Project

The Leftbank Building at 240 N Broadway is a 1923 A.E. Doyle design and one of only three remaining original buildings from Portland’s Albina neighborhood — a former warehouse whose nearly 100-year history runs through hospitality, manufacturing and music, including the Dude Ranch jazz club. Colliers needed a digital leasing brochure to market office and creative spaces from 500 to 6,500+ SF to tenants and their brokers: lead with the character of a seismically retrofitted landmark, and show a neighborhood on the rise.

[02]Solution

A landscape brochure that lets the building’s own materials carry it: brick, timber, vaulted ceilings and oversized windows in full-bleed photography, framed by black panels and a burnt-orange accent drawn from the brick. Corsario’s warm serif headlines give it a sense of history, Open Sans keeps the details plain, and a row of simple line icons turns the six key amenities — seismic retrofit, the warehouse-to-office conversion, an on-site café, Energy Star certification, the ceilings and windows, and transit, bike and parking access — into a scannable strip.

The pages move from the building outward: the cover and its amenity strip, the history spread, the spaces, a local-amenities map with bike and transit scores of 95 and 81, then an aerial of Lower Albina that maps the revitalization around the block — Albina Vision Trust, the I-5 Rose Quarter project, the Moda Center renovation and Lloyd Center redevelopment — before closing on the street view and the leasing team.
