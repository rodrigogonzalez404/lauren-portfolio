Kress Building

A digital leasing brochure for a fully modernized historic landmark in the heart of downtown Portland.

[Client]Colliers
[Type]Digital Leasing Brochure
[Location]Portland, OR
[Year]2026
[Role]Graphic Designer
[01]Project

The Kress Building at 630 SW 5th Avenue is a fully modernized historic landmark in the heart of downtown Portland’s retail and business core, with Nike and Sephora anchoring its ground floor. Colliers needed a digital leasing brochure to bring 20,300 SF of office space across the second and third floors — including a 10,000 SF spec suite under construction — to tenants and the brokers who represent them, and to make the case that a landmark can work like new: upgraded building systems, an excellent window line, a well-capitalized local owner and one of the city’s best-connected addresses.

[02]Solution

A landscape brochure in deep Kress green and warm parchment, with a single rust-orange accent kept for the message brokers must not miss. Cinzel’s inscriptional capitals give the headlines the presence of a landmark, Proxima Nova keeps every fact clean and modern, and a line-drawn rosette medallion — repeated large and tonal behind the cover — carries the identity from the first page to the last.

The pages read the way a tenant tours: the cover leads with three reasons to call — one block from the future James Beard Market, a well-capitalized local ownership group, a 10,000 SF spec suite under construction — then “A Landmark Redefined” sets out the renovation and the availability, a full-bleed street view declares “Where Business Moves”, two numbered maps prove the neighborhood and the parking, and floor plans for both floors plus a designed spec-suite layout close the case.
