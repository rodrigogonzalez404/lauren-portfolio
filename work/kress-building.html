Kress Building

A digital leasing brochure for a fully modernized historic landmark in the heart of downtown Portland.

[Client]Colliers
[Type]Digital Leasing Brochure
[Location]Portland, OR
[Year]2026
[Role]Graphic Designer
[01]Project

A fully modernized historic landmark in downtown Portland, with Nike and Sephora on the ground floor. Colliers needed a digital leasing brochure to market 20,300 SF of office space on the second and third floors, including a 10,000 SF spec suite.

[02]Solution

Deep Kress green and warm parchment with a single rust-orange accent, Cinzel capitals for the landmark and Proxima Nova for the facts, tied together by a line-drawn rosette medallion. The pages read like a tour: the renovation, the street, two neighborhood maps and the floor plans.
